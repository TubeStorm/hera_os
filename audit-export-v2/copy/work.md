AI-native workspace

HERA OS
A design experiment in making agentic AI visible, controllable, and safe for non-technical builders — memory, files, agents, permissions, and cost made legible.

The problem
AI tools break trust: users can’t see what the system remembers, what files exist, what agents did, or why it cost money.

What I designed
The trust layer of an AI workspace — a global assistant, room-scoped work, memory blocks, file-aware chat, and visible agent actions.

What it proves
I can architect an AI product’s invisible model — objects, states, and trust — end to end.

Enterprise internal tools
Senior UX Specialist — 2021→now
Ubisoft Internal Tools
Rebuilding trust in planning, staffing, and governance workflows used by 1,000+ people. A 101-response satisfaction survey and a 40+ modal audit turned inconsistent approvals, quotas, and policy into one coherent system.

The problem
RPM, approvals, and quota tools had drifted into 40+ inconsistent modals; long-time and frequent users trusted them least, so people routed around governance.

What I designed
A research-led systemization across three workstreams — an RPM modal + ribbon revamp, five structured approval flows, and warning-only quota visibility.

What it proves
I can tame high-stakes enterprise UX at scale and turn policy-heavy chaos into a usable, research-backed system.
