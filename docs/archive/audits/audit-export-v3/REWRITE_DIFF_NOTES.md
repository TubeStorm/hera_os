# Rewrite Diff Notes — 2026-07-04

Before/after receipts proving the rewrite fixed the right problem: generic AI-portfolio copy → specific, provable, Favour-voiced copy.

---

**Page: Home (hero)**
- Before: "I make AI systems easier to trust, control, and actually use." + "I turn messy AI behavior — permissions, memory, files, tasks, and user intent — into interfaces people can understand."
- After: "I design systems that make complicated work visible." + "My current work, HERA, explores what AI could feel like if it were less like a chatbot and more like a visible operating system for real work."
- Why better: the before could headline any AI UX portfolio; the after states a point of view and names the proof in the same breath.

**Page: Home (HERA section)**
- Before: "I designed the trust layer for an AI workspace… what the AI saw, did, saved, forgot, and cost…"
- After: "What if AI felt less like a chatbot and more like a place?" + real Void screenshot + three concrete bullets (rooms, event ledger, no fake $0.00).
- Why better: replaces an abstract "trust layer" diagram with the actual product, photographed.

**Page: HERA (heading)**
- Before: "HERA OS — Making agentic AI visible, controllable, and safe to trust"
- After: "HERA OS — an operating system where AI work is visible"
- Why better: the before is the exact phrase the preflight banned; the after says what the thing is.

**Page: HERA (opening)**
- Before: "AI tools promise leverage but often deliver fragility… You can't trust what you can't see."
- After: "Most AI tools still feel like text boxes attached to invisible machinery. You type, wait, and hope."
- Why better: plain thing first; the reader feels the problem instead of being told about trust again.

**Page: HERA (capability list → product systems)**
- Before: 12-item flat feature grid ("Composer 2.0", "Canonical chat integrity", …) with no reasons.
- After: 7 product systems, each with the why ("Iris used to be a room — that was wrong, and fixing it defined the whole model.") + 7 named proof modules.
- Why better: groups become an architecture; every group carries a decision or failure, not a label.

**Page: HERA (failures)**
- Before: 4 bullets, polished ("Ghost turns… pushed me to design explicit turn states").
- After: 7 failure cards including the embarrassing ones (the assistant blocked the app she was supposed to control; money left quietly).
- Why better: honest specificity is the credibility engine of the whole page.

**Page: Work**
- Before: "Two case studies in making complex software legible." (2 cards, Ubisoft collapsed into one mega-project)
- After: "One flagship product. Three enterprise workstreams." (HERA featured + RPM / Work Arrangement / Capacity Governance as separate linked projects)
- Why better: matches the resume's Selected Projects exactly; no more fake mega-case-study.

**Page: Enterprise (heading)**
- Before: "Ubisoft Internal Tools — Rebuilding trust in planning and governance workflows"
- After: "Enterprise Systems at Ubisoft — three workstreams in planning, approvals, and governance" + explicit "These are three distinct projects, not one."
- Why better: stops pretending; also stops leaning on "trust" as a heading noun.

**Page: Resume (headline + summary)**
- Before: "Senior UX / Product Designer — AI Workflows, Agentic Systems & Enterprise Tools" + summary repeating agentic/trust/architecture keywords.
- After: "Product Designer for AI systems, internal tools, and workflow-heavy products" + "I design systems where complicated work becomes visible and manageable…"
- Why better: reads like a person, scans in seconds, and no phrase appears twice.

**Page: Resume (HERA role)**
- Before: "Product Architect & UX Systems Designer / Owned product architecture, UX systems, agent workflow design, spec writing, and QA leadership."
- After: "Founder & Product Designer / Designed and built the product model for a local-first AI workspace… Built the working app solo: Python/FastAPI backend, React/TypeScript frontend, Phaser pixel world, SQLite event ledger."
- Why better: independent product work stated as fact, with a stack instead of a title pile.

**Page: About**
- Before: third-person-adjacent positioning ("My work sits where AI behavior meets human understanding").
- After: "AI should not feel like invisible magic. It should feel like a place you can understand, enter, and direct." + "…argue about backend event ledgers and sprite sheets in the same conversation."
- Why better: an actual thesis plus a detail only this person could write.

**Removed entirely**
- Home "What I design" 6-capability grid and "How I work" 4-step grid — both were label collections that repeated the case studies; their jobs are done better by the project map.
- The rotating role list "AI Product Systems Designer / Agentic AI UX / Product Architect / AI Workflow Product Builder".
