I'm Favour Diokpo, a UX and product systems designer. My work sits where AI behavior meets human understanding — **agentic AI UX, workflow automation, and internal tools**.

I'm most useful when a product is powerful but fragile: agents that act invisibly, permissions that interrupt, memory and files that quietly disappear. I turn that mess into **interfaces people can read, control, and rely on**.

Before AI-native software, I spent years inside enterprise internal tools — planning, staffing, and governance systems used by more than a thousand people. That taught me how to make dense, high-stakes software feel calm and trustworthy. I bring the same discipline to AI.

## How I think

Eyebrow: How I think
Title: **A few things I believe about AI UX.**

(Re-uses the six "What I design" capability items:)

1. **Agentic AI UX** — Interfaces that make autonomous agents legible — what they did, why, and what happens next.
2. **Workflow systems** — Turning messy multi-step automation into flows people can follow, pause, and trust.
3. **Internal tools** — High-density operational tools for real users doing real work at scale.
4. **Product architecture** — Naming the systems, states, and objects a product needs before pixels exist.
5. **Human-readable automation** — Making memory, files, tasks, and permissions readable to non-technical people.
6. **Safer AI interaction models** — Permission models and guardrails that protect users without interrupting their flow.
