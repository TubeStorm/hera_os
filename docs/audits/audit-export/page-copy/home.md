## How I work

Eyebrow: How I work
Title: **Quiet process, hard-to-break results.**

- **01 Map the system** — Before UI, I map objects, states, and edge cases — the invisible model users will feel.
- **02 Design for trust** — Every AI action gets a visible cause and a reversible path. No dead-ends, no silent failure.
- **03 Build & pressure-test** — I write specs, prototype flows, and QA the ghost states most teams ship by accident.
- **04 Refine relentlessly** — Ship, watch real behavior, cut friction. Quiet, confident, hard-to-break surfaces.
