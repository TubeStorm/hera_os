Run the Week 1 portfolio preflight.

OBJECTIVE

Determine the strongest, truthful way to extend the existing HERA/YC website into Favour's career portfolio before any new portfolio page is implemented.

The immediate portfolio surfaces are:

1. About
2. Ubisoft Work

This preflight must establish what already exists, what evidence is available, what is missing, what should be reused, and what the two pages should actually communicate.

DO NOT BUILD THE PAGES YET.

START FROM CURRENT PROJECT TRUTH

Read the project's persistent operating documents and current sprint state first.

Inspect the existing website repository and live portfolio structure deeply enough to understand:

* current routes and navigation;
* homepage purpose;
* HERA Deep Dive;
* Lineage;
* Contact;
* shared layouts/components;
* visual language;
* typography;
* spacing/layout patterns;
* responsive behaviour;
* assets already available;
* what should remain untouched.

Do not rediscover operating rules already recorded in project memory.

PORTFOLIO ARGUMENT

Evaluate the existing site against this intended professional story:

Favour is moving toward AI product systems work.

Her strongest demonstrated interests are:

* 0→1 product invention;
* designing system behaviour;
* human–AI interaction and autonomy boundaries;
* complex workflow architecture;
* conceptual product leadership.

HERA is the primary proof of original product thinking.

Ubisoft is the proof of:

* five years of professional reliability;
* senior-level systems UX work;
* breadth across internal tools;
* research and stakeholder leadership;
* working within large organizational and technical constraints.

This is NOT intended to become a conventional UI/visual-design portfolio.

PREFLIGHT QUESTIONS

Answer the following.

A. CURRENT SITE

1. What professional story does the current website tell today?
2. What is already strong enough to keep?
3. What currently makes the site read primarily as a YC/founder website rather than a career portfolio?
4. What existing visual/page patterns should About and Ubisoft Work reuse?
5. What should NOT be redesigned during this sprint?

B. ABOUT PAGE

Determine the best content architecture for a page whose real subject is:

“How Favour turns unclear ideas into coherent product systems.”

The page should demonstrate, where evidence supports it:

* what kinds of problems she naturally notices;
* how she questions inherited assumptions;
* how she finds the real product problem;
* how she creates mental models/product primitives;
* how she defines system behaviour;
* how she thinks about human versus AI authority;
* how she uses AI agents as researchers/builders/critics without outsourcing product judgment;
* how she plans and slices complicated work;
* how she verifies AI-assisted work;
* what decisions remain human;
* how this working method connects HERA and her professional experience.

Identify which claims can already be supported by repository evidence and which require Favour's personal explanation.

Do not write polished final copy yet.

C. UBISOFT WORK PAGE

Determine a breadth-first architecture.

The purpose is NOT to create exhaustive case studies for every Ubisoft project.

The page should demonstrate the range of systems Favour has worked on and then use a small number of focused examples to show her reasoning from unclear problem → investigation → product decision → clearer system.

Look for evidence already available in this repository/project materials around categories such as:

* resource planning;
* staffing/capacity;
* analytics/reporting;
* governance and approvals;
* data-heavy interfaces;
* shared patterns/design systems;
* operational/internal tools.

Separate all findings into:

* VERIFIED AND PUBLIC-SAFE;
* VERIFIED BUT PUBLIC SAFETY UNKNOWN;
* CLAIM REQUIRES FAVOUR;
* NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE.

Do not assume that an internal Ubisoft name, screenshot, metric or workflow is safe to publish merely because it exists somewhere accessible.

Recommend only 2–3 focused problem stories for deeper treatment.

D. HERA AS PROFESSIONAL EVIDENCE

Audit the existing public HERA material specifically for career relevance.

Identify:

* the strongest evidence of product invention;
* strongest evidence of system-behaviour design;
* strongest evidence of human–AI interaction thinking;
* strongest evidence of product leadership;
* strongest evidence of workflow architecture.

Also flag anything on the public HERA pages that may be unnecessarily detailed from an IP perspective.

Do not change or hide anything during this preflight.

E. EVIDENCE GAPS

Produce the smallest useful list of things only Favour can provide.

Examples may include:

* Ubisoft screenshots or sanitized visuals;
* names/categories of additional tools;
* project responsibilities not documented;
* before/after examples;
* outcomes;
* confidentiality decisions;
* stories that best demonstrate her method.

Do NOT give her a giant homework list.

Rank missing evidence as:

* REQUIRED FOR FIRST VERSION;
* HELPFUL LATER;
* NOT NEEDED THIS WEEK.

F. EXTERNAL RESEARCH

Decide whether external research would materially improve either page.

Do not perform broad external web research using coding-agent allowance by default.

If useful, create bounded research prompts in the project's research-request directory.

Possible research needs include:

* current AI product/system designer portfolio patterns;
* how senior/staff AI product designers present systems work;
* strong portfolio methods for NDA-heavy enterprise experience;
* what hiring managers need to understand from an About/process page.

Only create a research request when its answer will affect a real page decision.

G. SITE ARCHITECTURE

Recommend the final site navigation after adding the portfolio material.

Evaluate whether the current structure should become something similar to:

Home / HERA / Ubisoft Work / About / Lineage / Contact

but do not assume this exact order is correct.

Explain the hierarchy you recommend and why.

DELIVERABLES

Update the persistent project truth/current sprint documents where appropriate.

Create one preflight artifact containing:

1. Executive finding.
2. Current-site truth.
3. Portfolio positioning gap.
4. About page content architecture.
5. Ubisoft Work content architecture.

Do not turn the implementation slices into full technical packets yet.

FINAL RESPONSE TO FAVOUR

Keep the final response short and decision-oriented.

Tell her:

* what you learned;
* what you recommend;
* what you need from her now;
* what the first implementation slice should be.

Do not bury her under the repository audit.

STOP after the preflight.

Do not implement About.
Do not implement Ubisoft Work.
