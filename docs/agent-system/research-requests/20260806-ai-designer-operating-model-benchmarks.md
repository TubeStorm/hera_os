# Research Request: Senior/Staff AI Product Designer Operating Model Portfolio Benchmarks

## Why this matters
Favour's About page aims to showcase her unique working method: turning unclear ideas into coherent product systems using multi-agent workflows while maintaining strict human product authority. Benchmark data on how top-tier Staff AI Product Designers (e.g., at OpenAI, Anthropic, Linear, Figma) structure their process and About pages will ensure our architectural layout matches or exceeds 2026 industry hiring expectations.

## Exact questions
1. How do senior/staff AI product designers explain their human-in-the-loop philosophy and AI agency boundaries on portfolio process pages?

3. How do top AI system designers balance showcasing technical depth (RAG, prompt engineering, agent orchestration) with strategic product outcomes?

## Required source quality
Primary portfolio teardowns, design lead blogs, or staff designer case studies from top AI-first companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Linear, Figma, Vercel) from 2024-2026. Avoid low-quality template aggregators or generic UX blogs.

## Desired output format
- Concise bullet points grouped by question.
- 2-3 concrete case study/About page structural examples with key takeaways.
- Specific recommendations for Favour's About page section layout.
